Our Mentors

Dr. Brijesh Kumar has worked as an academician and researcher in Computer Applications for several years. He is currently the Dean of the School of Computer Applications at Manav Rachna International University, Faridabad. He is a Senior Member of IEEE, Member of ACM, and Life Member of CSI, ISTE, and CEGR. His expertise lies in Simulation and Modelling and Web Technologies.

Dr. Brijesh Kumar
Dean

Dr. Rashmi Agrawal has over 22 years of experience as an academician and researcher in Computer Applications. She is currently the Associate Dean at the Department of Computer Applications, Manav Rachna International Institute of Research and Studies, Faridabad. She is a Senior Member of IEEE, Professional Member of ACM, and Lifetime Member of the Computer Society of India. She is a book series editor for Wiley and CRC Press and has published over 80 research papers. She has received the Powerful Women of Haryana and Karmsu Kaushalam Puruskar awards.

Dr. Rashmi Agrawal
Associate Dean

Dr. Neerja Negi is an Associate Professor in the Computer Application Department at Manav Rachna International Institute of Research and Studies, Faridabad. She has over 16 years of teaching experience. She holds a PhD in Web service selection from Jaypee Institute of Information Technology and has qualified GATE with 91 percentile. Anjali Pandey is an Assistant Professor in the Computer Applications Department at Manav Rachna International Institute of Research & Studies (MRIIRS), Faridabad. She has over seven years of teaching experience and is currently pursuing her PhD at MRIIRS. She holds an M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering with a specialization in Computer Networks from J.C. Bose University, Faridabad, and a B.Tech in the same field. Her research interests include Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, and Networking. She has authored multiple research papers and participates in workshops and faculty development programs. She is also a member of ACM.
